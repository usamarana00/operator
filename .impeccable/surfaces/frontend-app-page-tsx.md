## Direction contract

THESIS: Keystone rejects the AI-dashboard shell; the app is one dispensing counter where multi-agent reasoning reads as ingredients measured into a remedy, not a spinner.

OWN-WORLD: Amber glass, brass, marble/cream ground, ink type; one carmine accent for urgent/overdue. Flat color fields, no gradients or glass chrome. Ledger/label face for names and status; clean grotesk for chat prose.

STORY: Visitor signs in (shop door), stocks the shelf (wizard), then works the counter daily: asks a question, watches PM/GitHub "ingredients" measured and compounded, receives a sealed labeled answer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Counter (chat) primary, shelf (project jars) as persistent side rail; agent-trace renders as a compounding sequence before the final labeled answer appears.

FORM: Apothecary/dispensary — my own top-ranked candidate (index 1 of 7), locked by the user over the dice-assigned index 6 (conservatory/greenhouse). Seed key f45e7596.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
